Womby — Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Womby collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, and makes no network requests. Everything you record stays on your phone.

What the app stores

When you tap the button, the app saves the time of that tap in a database on your own device. It also saves your settings — whether the screen stays on, whether it vibrates, whether it dims, and your language choice — and the moment you accepted the notice shown when you first opened the app, so that it is not shown again.

That is the whole of it. There is no name, no email address, no date of birth, no location, no device identifier, and no health data beyond the tap times you record yourself.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. The app contains no networking code at all: it cannot send your data anywhere, because there is nothing in it that can send anything. No analytics service, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK that could collect data on its own.

Who can see your data

Only you, on your device. Nobody — including the developer — receives it, stores it, or has any way to request it.

Your data may be included in a backup you have chosen to make of your whole phone (for example iCloud or Google backup). Those backups are governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies, not this one.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in the app and choose Delete all data. That removes every labor and every contraction from your device immediately and permanently; your settings are left as they are. Deleting the app removes everything, settings included.

Because none of it ever reaches us, there is nothing to request, export, or erase anywhere else.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Not a medical device

This app records the times you tap and shows you the patterns in them. It is a comfort and information tool, not a medical device, and it cannot tell you what is happening in your labor. It does not replace professional care. Follow the guidance of your midwife or doctor, whatever this app shows.
