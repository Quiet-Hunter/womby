Womby

A contraction timer for labor

One big button records when each contraction starts and ends. Womby shows three numbers at once: how long each contraction lasts, how often they come — from the start of one to the start of the next — and how much rest you actually have between them.

Your data

Everything you record stays on your phone. Womby has no account, no analytics, and makes no network requests. To delete everything, open Settings and choose Delete all data.

Privacy policy

Not a medical device

This app records the times you tap and shows you the patterns in them. It is a comfort and information tool, not a medical device, and it cannot tell you what is happening in your labor.

It does not replace professional care. Follow the guidance of your midwife or doctor, whatever this app shows.
